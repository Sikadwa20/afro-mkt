Terms of Service

1. Current service

Afro-Mkt provides seller accounts, monthly seller subscriptions and publicly visible product preview listings. Visitors cannot currently order or pay for products through Afro-Mkt. Buyer payment processing, delivery, returns for product orders and seller payouts will require separate arrangements before buyer transactions open.

A seller subscription provides listing access. It does not guarantee sales, visitors, revenue, placement, analytics or future features. Features advertised as coming later are not included as functioning launch services.

2. Accounts

You must be at least 18 and authorised to operate the seller account. Provide accurate details, verify your email, keep your login secure and notify us of suspected misuse. Use the same email for Stripe checkout and your seller account so your subscription can be matched.

3. Plans and renewal

Basic is €9.99 per month and allows up to 10 listings. Premium is €24.99 per month and allows unlimited listings subject to these rules and reasonable measures against spam or abuse. Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled. Stripe handles subscription payment processing. Review the final total, any applicable tax and renewal details shown at checkout before completing it; the amount shown at checkout is the amount you authorise.

Publication requires an active subscription. If the subscription ends or becomes inactive, listings may cease to appear publicly. Changing or closing a seller login does not itself cancel the Stripe subscription.

4. First-month launch promotion

The first 10 successful eligible discount redemptions across Basic and Premium receive 100% off the first monthly charge. The offer is applied automatically while available and expires on 31 October 2026. An incomplete checkout does not reserve a slot. The cap is shared across both plans and counts redemptions, not verified distinct people. After the free month, Basic renews at €9.99 per month or Premium at €24.99 per month unless cancelled before renewal. The offer does not provide a permanently free account.

Cancellation and refunds

Request cancellation at least 10 calendar days before your current monthly subscription period ends, by emailing support@afro-mkt.com from the email used at checkout. Include your seller email and, if available, your subscription reference. We will confirm the cancellation and its effective date.

For a timely request, cancellation stops the next renewal and access continues until the current billing period ends. Late requests are still accepted and processed as soon as possible. If another month has already been charged because the request was late, we will cancel further renewals and provide a partial refund for the unused days of that newly charged month, with access ending on the confirmed cancellation date.

The partial refund is calculated as the monthly amount paid × unused calendar days ÷ total calendar days in that charged billing period. We will explain the dates and calculation in the confirmation. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 14 days; your payment provider may take additional time to display the credit. A €0 promotional month has no paid amount to refund.

There is no routine refund for days already used or for a timely cancellation of the current month. Refunds for duplicate or incorrect charges, failure to provide the paid service, and any refund or withdrawal required by law remain available. The 10-day notice request does not reduce statutory rights.

Deleting an account, removing a listing or clearing browser data does not cancel billing. Request subscription cancellation explicitly. We will not ask for your password or full card number to process it.

Seller Agreement and listing rules

These rules supplement the Terms of Service and apply to every seller and listing.

Accurate product information

Use an accurate seller name, product title, EUR price, category, description and photo. Explain the product’s materials or ingredients, size or quantity, condition and intended use. Include relevant safety information, allergens, licences or other disclosures required for your product. Do not claim approval, certification, origin or performance you cannot substantiate.

Automatic publication

A new listing may publish automatically when your plan is active, its description contains at least 80 characters and 12 words, and the uploaded photo is valid and at least 800 × 800 pixels. JPG, PNG and WebP selections are prepared as PNG for technical checks, with a maximum prepared image size of 5 MB. The server checks the actual uploaded image. Listings that fail or cannot finish a check stay pending; the dashboard offers “Check for publication” to retry.

These checks do not detect every blurry photo, false claim, unsafe product or policy breach. Sellers must still use clear, representative images and comply with all rules. Edits reset approval and require a new check. Publication can later be restricted following a report or review.

Prohibited content

Do not list illegal, stolen, counterfeit, recalled or infringing goods; prohibited weapons or controlled drugs; unlawful hazardous products; sexually exploitative material; or hateful, abusive, fraudulent or deceptive content. Regulated products must meet their applicable requirements and licences. The presence of a food, beauty or other category is not permission to bypass those requirements.

Use photos and text you own or are authorised to use. Do not include private identity documents, payment details or unrelated personal information. Published seller names, listings and image URLs are public and can be copied or shared.

Reports and review

Report a listing by emailing support@afro-mkt.com. Include its URL or ID, explain the alleged illegality or rule breach, attach relevant evidence and provide a reply address when appropriate. Please state that the report is accurate to the best of your knowledge. We will assess reports and provide appropriate follow-up and reasons for restrictions where required by law.

Affected sellers can request human review by emailing the same address with the listing ID, their explanation and supporting evidence. We may request additional product information. Statutory routes to challenge a decision remain available.

No buyer orders or seller payouts are provided through Afro-Mkt during this stage. Buyer transactions will require updated payment, delivery, return and payout terms before they become available.
